# Acko General Insurance

> Acko (ACKO Technology & Services Private Limited) is an Indian digital-first general insurance company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Bengaluru. It sells car, bike, health, term life and travel insurance online, and is a portfolio company of Accel. Acko exposes insurance policy certificates to citizens through India's government API Setu / DigiLocker platform, and operates a separate enterprise partner API for embedded insurance issuance.

## APIs

- [Acko General Insurance (API Setu / DigiLocker)](https://apisetu.gov.in/acko/v3): Fetch Acko car, health and two-wheeler insurance policy certificates via India's government API Setu platform. Auth: X-APISETU-APIKEY + X-APISETU-CLIENTID headers.
- [Acko Enterprise API](https://www.acko.com/enterprise/documentation/enterprise.html): Partner/enterprise issuance API covering token generation, Loan-Shield, Trip, GIG, Health, House, Credit Life, Fire, Cyber and Electronics insurance products, plus policy/claims management.

## Specs

- [OpenAPI 3.0 (API Setu)](openapi/acko-apisetu-openapi-original.json): 3 operations — cripc (car), hlipc (health), twipc (two-wheeler) certificate fetch.
